Privacy Policy — UNU Task Sniper

Last updated: October 30, 2026

UNU Task Sniper is a browser extension that helps users find, book and manage tasks on unu.im.

Data the extension sends

DataSent toWhy
Activation key, or your UNU account number (for the free trial day)Our license server (Google Apps Script)To check that the key or trial is valid
A random device ID and the extension versionOur license serverTo limit each key to its devices and to offer updates
Daily counts of booked, finished and rejected tasks, and their total priceOur license serverUsage statistics for the key owner
A backup of your extension settings: filter profiles and the “My data” entries you add (for example your Telegram username)Our license server, linked to your activation key (or UNU account during the trial)To restore your settings when you install the extension on another device or reinstall it
Search phrases you add to your filters, and the titles of your “My data” entries (not their values)MyMemory translation API (api.mymemory.translated.net)To translate them into Russian

Data stored on your device only

Your booked tasks list, earnings history, report drafts and notes are kept in the browser's extension storage and are never sent anywhere.

Screenshots you take with the extension (the “📸” button in the popup or the optional floating button) are also kept only on your device. They are added to the report form on unu.im for you to send, and are deleted after the report is sent, when you remove them, or after 2 days.

Optional permission

If you turn on the floating screenshot button, the browser asks you to allow access to all websites. This is used only to show the button on the website of your task and to capture the visible page when you press it. Nothing from those websites is read or sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in the extension's settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your UNU password, cookies or browsing history. Apart from the screenshots you take yourself, the extension does not read data from websites other than unu.im.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or credit decisions.
  • The extension acts on unu.im only on the pages you open and with the settings you choose.
