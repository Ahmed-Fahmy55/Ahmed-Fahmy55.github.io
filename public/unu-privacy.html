Privacy Policy — UNU Task Sniper

Last updated: October 28, 2026

UNU Task Sniper is a browser extension that helps users find, book and manage tasks on unu.im.

Data the extension sends

DataSent toWhy
Activation key, or your UNU account number (for the free trial day)Our license server (Google Apps Script)To check that the key or trial is valid
A random device ID and the extension versionOur license serverTo limit each key to its devices and to offer updates
Daily counts of booked and finished tasksOur license serverUsage statistics for the key owner
Text of a task you choose to translateOur license serverTo translate the task into Arabic
Search phrases you add to your filtersMyMemory translation API (api.mymemory.translated.net)To translate your phrases into Russian

Data stored on your device only

Your settings, filter profiles, booked tasks list and notes are kept in the browser's extension storage and are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your UNU password, cookies, browsing history, or any data from websites other than unu.im.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or credit decisions.
  • The extension acts on unu.im only on the pages you open and with the settings you choose.
